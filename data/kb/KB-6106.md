# KB-6106: HX-5000 7.4 EtherChannel mis-bundles member ports when partner uses slow LACP timers

## Summary
On HX-5000 running 7.4, an EtherChannel can temporarily mis-bundle member links when the neighboring switch uses slow LACP timers. The bundle may appear partially formed, with two of four links active and the remaining members suspended until the condition clears or the interface is reinitialized.

## Symptoms
In affected systems, a 4-link LACP port-channel to a neighboring switch may establish with only 2 active members after a link flap or recovery event. The missing links typically remain suspended for 30 to 90 seconds, and the condition can recur during maintenance or when one member is bounced individually.

Operationally, `show lacp neighbor` may still report the partner as synchronized, which can make the bundle appear healthy at first glance. However, `show interfaces port-channel` shows repeated collect/distribute mismatch events, and the system may log %LACP-4-MISBUNDLE when the local port-channel rejects a member during the transient state.

## Diagnosis
1. Run `show interfaces port-channel 10` and verify whether the port-channel is up with fewer active members than physically connected. Affected bundles commonly show two active and two suspended members after a flap.
2. Run `show lacp neighbor` and check whether the partner is in sync state while the local side still reports suspended members. This combination indicates the issue is not a basic adjacency loss.
3. Run `show logging | include MISBUNDLE` and look for `%LACP-4-MISBUNDLE` events near the time of the flap or maintenance window.
4. Run `show interfaces status` on the member ports and confirm that the links are physically up but not all are participating in the bundle.
5. If available on the platform, run `debug lacp events` briefly during a controlled flap and confirm that collect/distribute is set before partner timeout confirmation is received. This ordering matches the defect.

## Root cause
In release 7.4, the LACP state machine can begin collecting and distributing traffic before it has confirmed the partner timeout bit when the peer is configured for slow LACP timers. During this window, the local switch treats the member as eligible for aggregation even though partner timer confirmation is still pending.

That timing gap creates a transient mismatch in the bundle state. The local system then suspends the member ports that should have joined the EtherChannel, producing a partial bundle and repeated collect/distribute mismatch events until the state machine converges or the port-channel is cleared.

## Workaround
1. If the design allows it, configure both ends of the bundle for fast LACP timers so timer confirmation converges more quickly.
2. If you cannot change timers immediately, clear the affected EtherChannel after the member links have restored to force the bundle to renegotiate cleanly.
3. During maintenance on a slow-timer bundle, avoid flapping individual member links one at a time; bring links up together where possible to reduce the mismatch window.
4. After applying the timer change or clearing the bundle, verify that all members transition to active state and that no new mismatch events are logged.

```text
configure terminal
interface port-channel 10
lacp rate fast
```

## Resolution
The defect is fixed in release 7.5. In that release, member ports wait for partner timer confirmation before entering collect/distribute, which prevents the transient aggregation mismatch seen in 7.4.
